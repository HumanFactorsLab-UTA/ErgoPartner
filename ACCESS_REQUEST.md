# Request research evaluation access

Email the Human Factors Lab at UTA or Dr. Rahman with the subject **ErgoPartner 5.1.0 evaluation request**. Send requests privately. [Human Factors Lab](mailto:hflab@uta.edu?subject=ErgoPartner%205.1.0%20evaluation%20request) · [Dr. Rahman](mailto:mrahman@uta.edu?subject=ErgoPartner%205.1.0%20evaluation%20request)

Include the following details in your message.

- Your name, affiliation if applicable, and contact email.
- The names of the evaluators and the version you are requesting.
- A short description of what you plan to evaluate, for how long, and whether you intend to conduct research or publish results.
- Any proposed modifications, integration, deployment or sharing that would need separate permission.

The lab will review your request and provide the applicable agreement. Permission begins when the rights holder confirms the named recipients, software version and scope in writing. Approved evaluators receive private download instructions. Send only the details needed for the request. Keep participant identifiers, recordings, case files, signatures and approval correspondence out of GitHub issues.

Review the [evaluation policy](USE_POLICY.md) before requesting access. Direct questions about request records or their retention to the lab.
